Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before your canyoning day. Still unsure? We are one message away.

Ask us on WhatsApp
Reply timeWithin 15 minutes, 08:00–20:00 WITA.
Do I need canyoning experience to join?
No. Egar and Maboya are beginner-friendly and include a full safety briefing. Samba and Aling are better suited to guests with some outdoor experience and comfortable swimming ability.
Is canyoning safe?
Canyoning is an adventure activity with inherent risks. We use checked equipment, trained local guides, safety briefings and water-level checks. We adjust or cancel routes when conditions are unsafe. Tell us about relevant medical conditions before booking.
Can I join if I cannot swim?
Egar and Maboya can be suitable for non-confident swimmers because swimming is optional on those routes. Samba and Aling require comfortable swimming ability. Please tell us honestly about your comfort in water when booking.
What happens if it rains?
We monitor weather and water levels. If conditions are unsafe, we reschedule your trip or offer a full refund. Light rain alone does not always cancel a trip; your guide will make the final call.
What is included in the price?
Prices include a local guide, canyoning equipment, safety briefing, lunch and drinking water. Most tours include edited photos and pickup from Ubud. Check each canyon for exact inclusions.
Do you offer hotel pickup?
Yes. Pickup is available from Ubud and selected areas of South Bali. Pickup outside Ubud may carry a surcharge. Confirm your area when booking and we will send the exact pickup time.
What should I bring and wear?
Bring swimwear, quick-dry clothes, water shoes or trainers, sunscreen, a towel and a change of clothes. We provide the canyoning harness, helmet and wetsuit.
How do I confirm and pay?
Send a booking request with your date and group size. We confirm availability within 15 minutes during business hours. You can then secure the date with a 25% deposit or pay the full amount by card, bank transfer or QRIS. The checkout on this demo site simulates payment only. Prices are in IDR.
What is your cancellation policy?
Free cancellation up to 48 hours before departure. Cancellations within 24–48 hours may incur a 50% charge. No-shows are charged in full. Weather cancellations are rescheduled or refunded. Read the full cancellation policy.
Can I book a private group?
Yes. Private groups are available on all canyons, subject to availability. Contact us on WhatsApp with your preferred date and group size for a private quote.
